## 2. The Gap

**What exists on Roblox:** dozens of *path/lane* auto-fight games (Anime Expeditions, Vanguards, UTDZ, ASTD, all TD derivatives) and one grid-placement variant that scaled (Garden Tower Defense). What exists on the *loop* side: Anime Squadron's "plan your lineup" pre-battle phase and Anime Card Clash's roll→deck→auto-fight, both of which reached only 6–8K CCU and neither of which sells **positional skill** as the core fantasy.

**What does not exist:** a Roblox game where **spatial arrangement on a bounded board is the primary skill expression and the primary progression axis**, married to an **Elo ladder with seasonal reset**. Every scaled title in the set resolves player agency into *which unit you own*, not *where you put it* — placement is a throughput puzzle against a fixed path, so the meta is a gacha meta. That is exactly why they decay: when the answer is "own the 0.005% unit," the game is over once you own it (Anime Defenders: 3.43B visits → 394 CCU).

**The unoccupied position:** *chess as the skill layer, gacha as the cosmetic/variant layer.* Four concrete openings:
1. **Board positioning as the win condition** — no scaled Roblox competitor uses adjacency/line-of-attack/flanking as the decision surface. Garden TD proves the grid is not a comprehension barrier for the audience; nobody has made the grid *matter*.
2. **Elo is native, and unclaimed in this genre.** RIVALS (202,868 CCU) and Blade Ball (13,344 CCU) prove Roblox kids accept seasonal rank resets; zero unit-collector auto-battlers in this set run a real ladder. Chess players already accept Elo as legitimate — you inherit the mental model free.
3. **Evergreen IP.** Every competitor above 100K CCU rents a meme (Skibidi, brainrot) or an anime license. Toilet TD's `[LEGACY]` tag is what renting looks like at end of lease. Chess costs nothing, ages never, and is globally pre-taught.
4. **Player-as-piece.** The brief's own hook — the avatar *is* the piece — is absent from every title in the set. It converts every cosmetic SKU into something the player literally embodies on the board, which is what makes cosmetic-first monetization viable (TDS's 4.9B-visit template).

**Honest counter:** the gap may be unoccupied because it doesn't sell. Battle Pet is the nearest structural attempt — 157,828 likes but **445 CCU**. Players enjoy the auto-battler shape and leave. The gap is real; the burden of proof is retention, not novelty.

## 3. Roblox-Specific Retention Hooks (ranked)

1. **Seasonal Elo ladder with hard reset + rank-gated cosmetic.** Proof: Blade Ball resets every player's Elo to Rookie at season start and gates unique Sword Skins behind Champion / global-leaderboard placement (13,344 CCU / 6.286B visits); RIVALS runs Seasons 0–3 at ~2–3mo each with rewards keyed to highest rank reached and soft placement carry-over (202,868 CCU / 84.76M favorites) (sources: https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=6035872082,4777817887 as of 2026-07-23; season mechanics from https://bladeball.fandom.com/wiki/Ranked and https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Ranked_Seasons — community wikis, mechanic corroborated, **no published uplift figure**). **Adapt:** 8-week seasons, full Elo reset, one rank-gated piece skin per season per tier. This is the most native-fitting hook in the entire set and it feeds the cosmetics line directly.
2. **Collection surface area / an index you can never finish.** Proof: Pet Simulator 99 — "✨ Currently 2,000+ pets to collect!" at 163,141 CCU / 2.558B visits; Sol's RNG — "400+ unique auras" at 31,010 CCU / 2.022B visits (source: https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=3317771874,5361032378, as of 2026-07-23). **Adapt:** 6 piece types × N skins × rarity tiers = a completion grid with a visible denominator. Ship the dex UI in v1; the number of collectibles *is* the retention surface.
3. **The pull itself as a loop, with luck sold as the accelerant.** Proof: Sol's RNG reduced the game to press-Roll-get-aura and still hit 2.02B visits; its dev description sells "potions to increase your luck". Anime Card Clash monetizes the same way via Luck / Cooldown tracks. **Adapt:** sell the **luck multiplier and the extra pull**, never the guaranteed unit. This is the single most important pattern in this document — it keeps the compulsion loop while dodging the pay-to-win read that will otherwise poison the ladder.
4. **Return-rate over raw scale — the algorithm rewards it.** Proof: The Battle Bricks (7,420 CCU, single-player unit-draft auto-battler, explicitly Battle Cats-inspired) surfaced in Roblox's own `top-revisited` sort — Roblox's algorithmic label for games players come back to (source: https://apis.roblox.com/explore-api/v1/get-sort-content?sortId=top-revisited, as of 2026-07-23). **Adapt:** optimize D1/D7 return, not CCU spikes. `top-revisited` placement is free distribution and it is the metric DevEx actually pays on.
5. **Daily login streak — table stakes, do not build it.** Roblox ships a first-party **Engagement Rewards** feature package with client+server logic, default UI, Time and Daily reward types, `isHiddenOnJoin` auto-popup, `isAlignedToStreakResetTime`, and a documented day-1→day-7 escalation example (source: https://create.roblox.com/docs/resources/feature-packages/engagement-rewards, as of 2026-07-23). ⚠ **The docs publish no retention statistics** — the uplift is inference. **Adapt:** drop the package in, spend zero engineering on it, spend all effort on reward *contents* (pull tickets, cosmetic shards).
6. **Trading — strongest long-tail hook, free to operate, highest regulatory risk.** Proof: Murder Mystery 2 (2014) at 296,610 CCU / 27.59B visits and Adopt Me! at 261,565 CCU / 44.20B visits are both trading economies; MM2's friction-light trade UX spawned an out-of-game value ecosystem (Traderie, Dexerto value lists) that pulls players back in (sources: https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=383310974,66654135 as of 2026-07-23; trade UX/value-list detail from secondary guides, **unverified**). **Adapt — with a hard constraint:** trading + paid gacha in one game is a loot-box-with-cash-out structure aimed at minors. If trading ships, **cosmetics only, and anything obtained from a paid pull is permanently untradeable.**
7. **Timed multiplier events on a visible cadence.** Weakest-evidenced tier here (Brainrot TD bakes "2x diamond" into its title at 693 CCU; Grow a Garden's published weekly calendar was cited but arrived truncated — treat as **unverified**). **Adapt:** publish a weekly event calendar out-of-game; cheap to run, gives Discord/YouTube something to cover.

## 4. Monetization Playbook

**Base SKUs (ship all of these regardless of the gacha decision):**
- **Piece skins** — the core cosmetic. Player-as-piece means every skin is worn, seen by opponents, and visible on the board every round. Tiered crates on the TDS model (Basic buyable with earned currency, Premium/Deluxe with Robux), with published rarity tables.
- **Board / VFX / capture-animation skins** — second cosmetic axis, near-zero balance risk.
- **Season Pass** (~400–800 Robux) keyed to the 8-week Elo season — recurring revenue that scales with the retention hook rather than fighting it.
- **Luck & extra-pull SKUs** — the Anime Card Clash / Sol's RNG pattern. Sells *odds*, not outcomes.
- **Event-exclusive crates that never re-release** — TDS's Bunny/Spooky FOMO mechanic, 7 years of evidence.
- **Roblox Premium payout bonus** — Anime Card Clash grants Premium players Luck/Cooldown bonuses; free incremental revenue via Premium Payouts.

### The Gacha Decision

**(a) Cosmetics-only gacha.** Evidence for: Tower Defense Simulator, cosmetic-first crates, **7 years, 4.865B visits, and a *stable* 21,357 CCU in 2026**. That is the only title in this entire set that has neither collapsed nor spiked — cosmetic-only correlates with flat, durable revenue. Evidence against: ARPDAU ceiling is materially lower, and Anime Card Collection (142M visits in ~7 months on pure pull-and-collect) shows a weak pull layer loses the acquisition race outright.

**(b) Units-in-gacha with F2P grind + pity + published odds.** Evidence for: Anime Vanguards, 2.0B visits / 1.97M favorites, with a verified 0.005% chase rate and a 20,000-summon one-time pity — that is the shape this audience tolerates and pays into. Evidence against, and it is severe: Anime Defenders sits at **394 CCU on 3.430B lifetime visits**; All Star Tower Defense at ~1,395 CCU on 7.875B. Unit-gacha games monetize violently then die. DevEx income tracks the spike years, not the tail — which is *tolerable* if you accept a 12–24 month revenue window, and fatal if you want a business.

**Recommendation: (b), with a mandatory competitive carve-out.**

Run unit gacha — pulls yield fighting pieces, with rarity tiers, pity, and a guaranteed F2P grind path — **but scope paid power strictly to the PvE ladder.** Ranked PvP draws from a **normalized shared pool**: every player enters ranked with the full roster available, so Elo measures positioning, never wallet. Sell luck, pull volume, cosmetics, and the Season Pass into that structure.

Justification:
- Cosmetics-only (a) is the safe answer and it caps you near TDS's profile — 21K CCU after *seven years* of live-ops. The brief asks for real DevEx income, and the acquisition data (Anime Card Collection, Sol's RNG) says the pull *is* the acquisition engine on Roblox. Shipping without a unit pull surrenders the top of the funnel.
- The decay in (b) is not caused by unit gacha per se; it is caused by **the gacha being the entire meta**. When owning the 0.005% unit is the win condition, completing the collection is the exit. A chess ladder gives players a reason to log in that gacha cannot exhaust — and the seasonal reset (Blade Ball / RIVALS pattern) manufactures a re-grind the roster alone never provides. This is the specific mechanism that decouples you from the Anime Defenders curve.
- The carve-out neutralizes the P2W read where it costs most. A ranked ladder that money can buy is worthless as a retention hook, and worthless as a marketing story.

**Regulatory work required for (b) — non-negotiable:**
- **Published probability disclosure.** Korea's Game Industry Promotion Act probability-disclosure mandate requires in-game and store-page odds disclosure for paid random items [Medium confidence — statutory requirement, **not verified in this research pass; get legal review before launch**]. Build the odds table as data, render it in-client, and version it per banner — retrofitting this later is expensive.
- **Pity must be published, not just implemented.** Anime Vanguards's disclosed 20,000-summon pity is what makes a 0.005% rate socially acceptable.
- **Paid-pull items are permanently untradeable.** Non-negotiable if trading ever ships (see hook #6).
- **No Robux→tradeable-value path, ever.** That is the structure regulators target hardest with a minor audience.

## 5. Minimal Addictive Core Loop

The MVP's only job is to answer one question: *does board positioning produce a compulsion to replay?* Everything that is not that question is cut — no gacha, no shop, no PvP, no seasons, no meta-progression beyond a single visible dex counter. Ship a single-player PvE run: the player drafts from a small offered set of chess pieces, places them on a bounded grid, presses Fight, watches a fully deterministic auto-resolve, and either advances to a harder wave or dies and restarts. Draft offers are randomized per round so the positioning problem is never the same twice — that RNG is the pull mechanic, delivered free, which is the cheapest possible test of whether the compulsion exists before any Robux code is written. Instrument exactly three things: **rounds per session, restart-after-loss rate, and D1/D7 return.** If restart-after-loss is weak, positioning is not fun and no monetization layer will save it — kill the project there rather than after the economy is built. Benchmark against Battle Pet's failure signature (high likes, 445 CCU): loved on first contact, abandoned by day two.

**The loop:**
- **Offer** — 3 random pieces from a pool of ~12, player picks 1 (this is the pull, unmonetized)
- **Place** — position owned pieces on a small grid (start 5×5); adjacency and line-of-attack must visibly change outcomes
- **Fight** — deterministic auto-resolve, ~20–30s, no player input
- **Reward** — gold + a dex entry unlock on win; visible "N / total collected" counter
- **Escalate** — next wave is harder; run ends on loss
- **Restart** — one tap, back to Offer with a fresh randomized pool

Skipped: gacha, Robux SKUs, PvP, Elo, seasons, trading, daily rewards. Add gacha and the ladder only after restart-after-loss and D7 clear the bar — the Roblox Engagement Rewards package covers dailies in an afternoon whenever you want it.

## 6. Top Risks

- **Gacha decay is the base case, not the tail risk.** Anime Defenders: 3.430B visits → **394 CCU**. All Star Tower Defense: 7.875B visits → ~1,395 CCU. Anime Vanguards: 2.0B visits → 10,060 CCU. Every unit-gacha title in this set is on the same curve. The seasonal-Elo layer is the *only* proposed mitigation, and it is unproven in combination with unit gacha on Roblox. If it fails, you get a 12–18 month revenue window and then a `[LEGACY]` tag.
- **Retention, not acquisition, is the killer — and the nearest structural comp already failed it.** Battle Pet: 157,828 likes, 23.4M visits, **445 CCU**. Players like auto-battlers on contact and do not stay. Assume you will reproduce this unless the MVP metrics say otherwise.
- **Pay-to-win poisons the one native hook.** Chess's entire value here is that Elo is credible. Any leakage of paid power into ranked destroys that credibility, and with it the seasonal-reset engine, the leaderboard, and the reason to log in on day 60. The normalized-pool carve-out must be a launch constraint, not a v2 fix.
- **Regulation on units-in-gacha with a minor audience.** Korean probability-disclosure obligations [Medium, unverified — needs legal review], plus loot-box scrutiny generally, plus Roblox's own policy surface. The lethal combination is paid gacha + trading; if both ship, paid-pull items must be permanently untradeable. Budget real legal time, and build the odds table as versioned data from day one.
- **Discovery in a saturated niche.** Anime Paradox X (1,137 CCU) is the median 2025-cohort outcome; Anime Squadron (~8K CCU in <1yr) is a good one; Anime Expeditions' 180,904 CCU is a **peak snapshot** and arithmetically inconsistent with its own visit history [Medium] — do not put it in a forecast. Chess has no meme velocity, so paid UGC/influencer distribution and `top-revisited` placement carry the whole load.
- **Design risk on the load-bearing assumption.** The claim that Garden Tower Defense proves grid placement works for this audience is an **unverified gameplay reading** of a game whose metrics were confirmed but whose mechanics were not. Play it and confirm before the board design depends on it — and separately validate that *positioning* (not just grid placement) reads as fun to a 10-year-old, which no title in this set demonstrates.
